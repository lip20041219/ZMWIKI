Project Orion is Acme Corp's data analytics platform project, initiated in mid-2025. The project is led by David Kim, a principal engineer, with Carol Chen contributing to the backend integration layer. Project Orion has a budget of three hundred thousand dollars and is expected to launch in Q2 2027. The platform aims to provide real-time business intelligence dashboards for all departments. The tech stack includes a columnar database, a stream processing engine, and a React-based frontend. David Kim reports to Eve Martinez.
