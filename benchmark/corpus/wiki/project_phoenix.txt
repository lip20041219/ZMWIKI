Project Phoenix is Acme Corp's internal platform migration initiative, launched in early 2025. The project is led by Alice Smith, with Carol Chen as a core team member. It has an allocated budget of five hundred thousand dollars and a target completion date of December 31, 2026. The goal of Project Phoenix is to migrate the company's legacy monolithic application to a microservices architecture running on Kubernetes. The project team meets every Tuesday and Thursday for standups and architecture reviews.
