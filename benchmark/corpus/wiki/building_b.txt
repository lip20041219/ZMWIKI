Building B is the second office building at Acme Corp, housing the executive and data engineering groups. It has five floors. The first floor features a fully equipped gym with locker rooms and showers, open to all employees. The second and third floors contain workspace for the data engineering team. The fourth floor houses David Kim's office and several meeting rooms. The fifth floor is the executive level, containing the VP of Engineering's office (currently Eve Martinez), the CEO's office, and a boardroom. Building B is connected to Building A by a covered walkway on the second floor.
