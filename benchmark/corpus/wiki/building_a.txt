Building A is one of two office buildings occupied by Acme Corp. It houses the platform engineering group and has three floors. The first floor contains open workspace for staff and senior engineers, including Carol Chen's desk. The second floor has additional offices and a small kitchenette; Alice Smith's office is on this floor. The third floor houses the Director of Engineering's office, currently occupied by Bob Jones, plus two conference rooms. A cafeteria serving breakfast and lunch is located on the second floor, adjacent to the kitchenette.
