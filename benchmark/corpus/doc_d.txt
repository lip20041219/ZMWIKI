Vector search retrieves items by the meaning of their representation rather than exact words. Each item is mapped to an embedding vector, a list of numbers that capture semantics. Similar items have vectors that point in similar directions, measured by cosine similarity. The goal of a nearest neighbor search is to find the stored vectors closest to the query vector. Approximate indexes trade a little recall for much faster search at scale. This approach powers recommendation, deduplication, and retrieval over unstructured data.
